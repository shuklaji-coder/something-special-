# I Am So Sorry, Akansha ❤️ (The Diet Coke & Mummu Treaty)

An ultra-cute, heartfelt, and irresistible romantic **Apology ("Sorry / Manane Wali") Website** dedicated specially to **Akansha Mishra** by **Rohan Shukla** (*"Your Favourite Mummu"*, Software Engineer).

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Interactive Experience

1. **Heartfelt Apology Hero & Letter**:
   - Borivali to Dahisar distance context (2 km / 5 mins away).
   - Cute puppy-eyes animated avatar with folded hands praying for forgiveness.
   - Sincere, genuine apology letter admitting full fault with no excuses.
   - Dedicated highlight on Akansha's favourite treat: **Diet Coke** 🥤!

2. **Interactive 3D Chilled Diet Coke Peace Offering**:
   - Realistic 3D metallic Diet Coke can with condensation droplets, silver sheen, and red banner.
   - **Tap to Pop**: Plays realistic pop audio & fizz hiss (via Web Audio API), shakes the can, and launches a burst of fizz bubbles & floating hearts.
   - Unlocks the *Lifetime Unlimited Diet Coke Treaty* banner!

3. **Mummu's 6 Sacred Promises (Interactive Cards)**:
   - *Galti Meri Thi, 100% Accept Karta Hoon* 💔
   - *Lifetime Unlimited Diet Coke Treaty* 🥤
   - *Tumhari Smile Meri Duniya Hai* 🥺
   - *Sirf 5 Minute Ki Doori (2 km)* 🛵
   - *Pinky Promise From Mummu* 🤞
   - *You Deserve The Best* 👑

4. **Akansha's Photo & Video Showcase**:
   - Beautiful memories featuring `photo1.jpg`, `photo2.jpg`, and `photo3.jpg`.
   - Romantic video reel player with smooth controls.

5. **Interactive Scratch Card**:
   - Metallic scratch foil on canvas with instruction text.
   - Rub/scratch with finger or mouse to reveal *Mummu's Secret Apology Contract*.
   - Auto-clears and celebrates when > 40% is scratched!

6. **The Runaway "NO" Button Climax**:
   - Glowing "YES, Maaf Kiya! ❤️" vs Runaway "No 😤" button.
   - Runaway button teleports away on hover / touch, shrinks, and cycles through funny excuses:
     - *"Pakka? 🥺"*
     - *"Mummu sach me ro dega 😭"*
     - *"Diet Coke chilled wali dunga, pakka! 🥤"*
     - *"Ek tight hug bhi dunga! 🫂"*
   - After multiple attempts, it playfully disables, leaving ONLY the glowing YES button!

7. **The Grand Celebration & WhatsApp Share**:
   - Camera flash effect + canvas fireworks display + multi-color confetti cannons.
   - Celebration modal confirming *Chilled Diet Coke Dispatched from Borivali to Dahisar*!
   - 1-click WhatsApp button to send a pre-filled confirmation message to Mummu.
