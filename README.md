# A Love Story for Akansha Mishra ❤️

An ultra-premium, cinematic, mobile-first proposal website handcrafted with romantic Bollywood aesthetics, Awwwards-level animations, 3D tilt effects, interactive chat simulation, handwritten letter unfold, and the runaway "NO" proposal climax.

---

## 🌟 Included Features & Experience Flow

1. **Chapter 0: Self-Drawing Heart Loader**
   - SVG stroke-dash animation drawing a glowing heart with percentage ticker: *"Kuch bohot special load ho raha hai Akansha ke liye..."*

2. **Chapter 1: "Hum Kabhi Mile Nahi..." (Intro)**
   - Starry night sky with drifting fireflies and falling 3D rose petals.
   - Poetic lines revealing one-by-one.
   - Automatic direct playback of the romantic song (*"Dil Ye Bekarar Kyun Hai"*) as soon as the page loads or on the very first touch/scroll on screen, without needing to press any "Play Music" button.

3. **Chapter 2: "Jab Tum Mili" (Interactive Chat Replay)**
   - Simulated WhatsApp/iMessage glassmorphism frame with Akansha's verified DP, typing indicators (`...`), and timed message bubbles replaying how late-night talks began.
   - Replay button to watch anytime.

4. **Chapter 3: "Tumhare Baare Mein" (3D Tilt Cards)**
   - 6 Glassmorphism cards with mouse tilt and mobile gyroscope/orientation support:
     - *Teri Qatilana Muskaan* (photo1)
     - *Teri Gehari Aankhein* (photo2)
     - *Your Grace & Elegance* (photo3)
     - *Teri Wo Pyaari Aawaz* (interactive voice note player mockup)
     - *Choti Choti Baaton Pe Care*
     - *Tumhare Pyaare Nakhre & 'Hmmm'*

5. **Chapter 4: "Meri Duniya Ki Jhalak" (Polaroids & Cinematic Video)**
   - Hanging polaroids swaying gently with realistic physics on a fairy-light wire.
   - Cinematic embedded video player (`video.mp4`) with luxury glass frame, ambient back-glow, and tap-to-unmute badge.

6. **Chapter 5: "Humari Doori" (Distance Map)**
   - SVG flight trajectory with a pulsing neon heart travelling between both cities.
   - Live counter: *"Humare beech 550+ km hain, par dil ke beech 0.00 mm."*
   - Audio & video call hours, messages sent, and 24/7 love counters.

7. **Chapter 6: "Jab Pehli Baar Milenge" (Sunset Dream Plan)**
   - Romantic sunset gradient scene with silhouettes and a 4-step first date plan.

8. **Chapter 7: "Khat-e-Dil" (3D Sealed Envelope)**
   - Realistic wax-sealed envelope.
   - Tap the seal to break open: the flap rotates in 3D, and the parchment letter unrolls with a live handwriting typewriter effect.

9. **Chapter 8: The Climax Proposal & Runaway "NO" Button**
   - Screen dims with a focused spotlight and an authentic Web Audio API heartbeat rhythm syncing with the vignette pulse.
   - Question: *"Akansha Mishra... Kya tum meri girlfriend banogi?"*
   - **YES Button**: Glowing rose-gold, pulsing in rhythm, grows larger with every NO attempt. Triggers camera flash, fireworks, and heart confetti cascade.
   - **NO Button**: Impossible to catch! Teleports away on mouseover/touch, shrinks, and changes funny Hinglish excuses (*"Are you sure? 🥺"*, *"Soch lo ek baar..."*, *"Pakka? Dil toot jayega mera 💔"*, *"Ab to YES hi dabana padega 😏"*). Vanishes completely after 8 attempts.
   - **Celebration Screen**: Photo collage of Akansha, *"She Said YES! Ab to milna padega!"*, and direct 1-tap WhatsApp sharing link.

10. **Easter Egg**:
    - Tap the top heart logo 5 times to reveal a secret love note with flying kiss emojis!

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

### Option 1: Just Double Click
Simply double-click `index.html` in any browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Brave).

### Option 2: Live Local Server
Run with Node.js:
```bash
npx serve .
```
Then open `http://localhost:3000` in your browser.

## 🎨 How to Customize

All texts, names, cities, and paths are located in `config.js`. You can edit anything there anytime:
- `herName`: Her name
- `myName`: Your name
- `herCity` / `myCity`: Your cities
- `distanceKm`: Distance in km
- `chatMessages`: The messages in Chapter 1
- `letter`: The paragraphs inside the Love Letter
